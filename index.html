About

I'm a software architect and technical leader with 20+ years building and scaling complex, distributed systems — across cloud, mobile and large multi-tenant platforms.

Today I'm a Cisco Webex architect and thought leader across Webex Wholesale and Webex for BroadWorks, owning key features along with scaling, security and performance. I set architecture standards and drive cross-cutting technical decisions for always-on platforms that serve carrier and SMB partners worldwide.

I'm a bottom-up thinker — comfortable from open source at the cloud-OS level, up through Kubernetes, database and queuing, to the service application-layer building blocks. That full-stack view lets me find the simplest solution that scales, rather than the most complicated one.

I work closely with PM, UX, engineering managers and technical leaders, and I mentor software engineers — turning partner and product requirements into architecture that delivers a delightful experience, built to be maintained and built upon.
